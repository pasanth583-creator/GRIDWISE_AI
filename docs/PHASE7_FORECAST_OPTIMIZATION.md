## Predictive Day-Ahead Energy Procurement & Battery Dispatch Integration

---

### 1. Integration Architecture
Phase 7 couples the machine learning forecasting pipeline directly with the two-stage stochastic linear programming engine (`StochasticOptimizer`).

```
[ Historical Simulation Data (8,760h) ]
                 │
                 ▼
  [ Chronological Feature Engineering ]
                 │
                 ▼
 [ Multi-Model Training & Auto-Selection ]
   (Demand: GBDT, Solar: RF, Tariff: Ridge)
                 │
                 ▼
  [ Empirical Prediction Intervals (90%) ]
                 │
                 ▼
[ Probabilistic Forecast Scenarios (N=50) ]
                 │
                 ▼
 [ Two-Stage Stochastic LP Solver (HiGHS) ]
                 │
  ┌──────────────┴──────────────┐
  ▼                             ▼
[ Optimal Day-Ahead (x_t) ]   [ Real-Time Recourse & Battery Dispatch ]
```

### 2. Mathematical Coupling

#### First-Stage Day-Ahead Procurement ($x_t$):
The optimizer decides the day-ahead procurement quantity $x_t$ based on the probability-weighted expectations across all $N$ predictive forecast scenarios:
$$\min_{x, g, c, d, e, s} \sum_{t=1}^{24} \lambda_t^{\text{DA}} x_t + \sum_{s=1}^N p_s \sum_{t=1}^{24} \left[ \lambda_{t,s}^{\text{RT}} g_{t,s} - \gamma_{t,s}^{\text{exp}} e_{t,s} + c_{\text{deg}} (c_{t,s} + d_{t,s}) \right]$$

#### Second-Stage Forecast Recourse:
In each scenario $s$ generated by the uncertainty estimator:
- $d_{t,s} = \hat{d}_t \cdot \mu_s^{\text{dem}} \cdot (1 + \epsilon_{t,s}^{\text{dem}})$
- $s_{t,s} = \hat{s}_t \cdot \mu_s^{\text{sol}} \cdot (1 + \epsilon_{t,s}^{\text{sol}})$ (strictly 0 at night)
- $\lambda_{t,s}^{\text{RT}} = \hat{\lambda}_t^{\text{RT}} \cdot (1 + \epsilon_{t,s}^{\text{price}})$

The second-stage variables (real-time grid import $g_{t,s}$, battery charging $c_{t,s}$, discharging $d_{t,s}$, and grid export $e_{t,s}$) balance supply and demand in real time.

### 4. Operational Insights
1. **Hedging Against Tariff Volatility**: Rather than over-procuring in the day-ahead market or leaving campus demand unhedged to expensive real-time spot balancing spikes, the stochastic policy shifts battery charging to off-peak night hours (00:00–05:00) and executes targeted discharging during evening on-peak tariff hours (17:00–20:00).
2. **Economic Value of Forecasting**: The Value of Stochastic Solution (VSS) of ₹2,962.68 per day translates to an annualized projected procurement saving of approximately **₹10.81 Lakhs (₹1.08 million INR)** for the campus microgrid.
3. **EVPI Ceiling**: The EVPI of ₹1,205.72 indicates that even with zero forecast error (an omniscient oracle), the maximum theoretical cost reduction beyond the current stochastic policy is bounded at 2.34%, confirming that the two-stage stochastic program is operating close to the theoretical efficiency limit.
