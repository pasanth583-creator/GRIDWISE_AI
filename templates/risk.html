Mathematical Policy

Risk-Neutral Policy (λ = 0.0)

Minimizes probability-weighted expected cost: min E[Cost]. Yields optimal routine operating costs (₹52,227.39), but leaves campus exposed to worst-case tail spikes (₹62,173.94) during low solar irradiance.

Mean-CVaR Hedged Policy (λ = 0.25 – 0.50)

Minimizes convex combination: min (1 - λ)·E[Cost] + λ·CVaR_α[Cost]. Procures firmer day-ahead reservations (2,829.0 kWh) and reserves battery charge, reducing tail risk exposure by ₹95.81 at a nominal premium of ₹39.55.

Maximum Tail Hedging (λ = 1.0)

Focuses exclusively on bounding worst 5% disaster scenarios. Increases forward procurement to 3,913.5 kWh, capping tail risk at ₹62,000.03.

Campus Recommendation: λ = 0.25 to 0.50 strikes the ideal balance between low routine utility spend and regulatory peak containment.
